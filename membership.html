Questions people actually ask

Which 25 or 50 funds do I get?

A spread across managers and both markets, drawn for you rather than picked by us — so the sample is not just the biggest names. It stays the same each time you come back, and creating an account draws a fresh, larger set.

What happens to my saved holdings if I cancel Pro?

Nothing is deleted. Your account reverts to the free limits, so the funds beyond them stop opening, and everything reappears if you subscribe again.

Is my portfolio connected to my brokerage?

No. Licentia has no link to any brokerage, cannot see your real holdings and cannot place trades. Anything in My Portfolio is there because you typed it in.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, from your account page, and access runs to the end of the period you have already paid for. No email required, no retention call.
