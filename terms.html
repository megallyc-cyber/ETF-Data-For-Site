What Licentia is

Where the numbers come from, and how wrong they can be

Fund data is gathered automatically from the fund issuers' own pages and refreshed on a schedule. Prices come from public market data. This means:

  • Figures may be stale, delayed, or missing entirely for a given fund.
  • An issuer may change its page and our reading of it may silently break.
  • Yields shown are trailing twelve months — what a fund has paid, not what it will pay.
  • Distributions can be cut, suspended, or partly a return of your own capital.

Always confirm anything that matters against the issuer's own documents before you act on it.

The portfolio and backtesting tools

The builder and the stress test are arithmetic on the figures we hold. They are not forecasts and they are not simulations of the future. A scenario labelled "severe bear" is a calculation of what today's positions would be worth after a given price move, nothing more. Past distributions and past returns tell you what happened; they do not tell you what will happen.
