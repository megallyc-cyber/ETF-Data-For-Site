Privacy

What Licentia knows about you

Short version: you can use the whole site without an account. If you make one, we keep the few things you save and nothing else. We do not sell anything to anyone.

Last updated September 1, 2026

Using the site without an account

Every research page — the fund directory, the comparison tool, the portfolio builder, backtesting and the learning pages — works without signing in. If you never create an account, we hold no personal information about you at all.

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the requests your browser makes, including your IP address, as a normal part of delivering pages. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them.

What an account stores

Accounts exist so your work follows you between devices. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email address, so you can sign back in.
  • A display name and picture, if you choose to set them. The picture is resized in your browser and stored against your account.
  • Holdings you save in My Portfolio: ticker, number of units, and the cost per unit if you enter one.
  • Funds you watch and portfolios you build.

That is the complete list. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number, date of birth, or any financial account details, and there is nowhere on the site to enter them.

We are not connected to your brokerage. Licentia has no link to any brokerage or bank, cannot see your real holdings, and cannot place trades. Anything in My Portfolio is there because you typed it in.

Signing in with Google

If you use the Google option, Google tells us your email address and, if available, your name and profile picture. We never receive your Google password, and we ask for nothing beyond basic profile information. You can use an email address and password instead if you prefer.

Who else touches your data

Two companies are involved in running the site:

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved portfolios. Our database is hosted in Canada.
  • GitHub serves the pages themselves.

Neither is given your information for their own purposes. Nobody else receives it. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and there are no advertisers involved in this site.

Analytics and tracking

There are no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts and no cross-site tracking pixels on Licentia. The site sets no cookies of its own. Signing in stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in, which is deleted when you sign out.

Getting your data out, or deleting it

You can remove any holding from My Portfolio at any time, and doing so deletes it. If you want your entire account and everything in it erased, or a copy of what we hold, email megallyc@gmail.com and it will be done. No reason is required.

Security, honestly stated

Accounts and saved data sit behind Supabase's authentication, and each account can only read its own rows. Traffic is served over HTTPS. That said, Licentia is a small, independent site rather than a bank, and no system is beyond compromise. Please do not store anything here that would harm you if it were exposed.

Children

Licentia is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly keep information about children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, the date at the top will change and the previous behaviour will not be applied retroactively to data already stored.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to see or delete your data: megallyc@gmail.com.
